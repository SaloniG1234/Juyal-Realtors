Before you call

Common questions

Do you charge for a consultation?

The first conversation about your requirement is a consultation, not a transaction. Brokerage and any pass-through charges are agreed in writing before a mandate begins.

Can you handle a YEIDA transfer end to end?

Yes — verification, transfer formalities with the authority, agreement staging and mutation follow-through. Bring the allotment papers before any advance is paid.

I am outside NCR. Can we meet online?

Online appointments run seven days a week, and site visits can be recorded and shared before you travel.

I want to sell or lease out. Where do you start?

With a realistic valuation, then a targeted approach to our vetted buyer and corporate tenant network — not a public listing free-for-all.
