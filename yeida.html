Before you pay anything

The authority plot checklist

Run every YEIDA resale through this list. We do it on every file we handle — and we will walk you through it even if you buy elsewhere.

Get this checked for your plot
  • Allotment letter in the seller's name — matched against the authority record, not just a photocopy.
  • Dues and instalment position — outstanding premium, interest, and any penalty cleared or priced into the deal.
  • Possession status — whether physical possession has been handed over, and what has actually been built around the plot.
  • Transfer eligibility — lock-in period, transfer charges, and whether this specific allotment can legally change hands now.
  • Plot identification on ground — the plot you are shown is the plot on the layout. Verified physically.
  • Encumbrance and litigation check — mortgage, dispute or attachment against the allotment.
  • Agreement structure — payment staged against transfer milestones, not released on trust.
  • Mutation follow-through — the file is not finished at payment; it is finished when the record shows your name.
