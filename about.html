About the firm

A one-desk consultancy with a two-decade memory

Juyal Realtors has operated from Sector 18, Noida since 2005. In a market that rewards volume and churn, the firm stayed deliberately boutique: fewer mandates, handled personally, with the paperwork treated as the product.

The principal

Yogesh Juyal

Founder & Principal Consultant

Mr. Juyal founded the practice in 2005 and still runs every mandate himself — the first consultation, the site visits, the negotiation and the closing. Clients across public review platforms name him personally, which is unusual in a market where most firms are known only by a brand.

His working method is consistent: understand the budget and the constraint before showing anything, be candid about what a corridor can realistically support, and put the documentation ahead of the deal. That approach is why the firm's reputation rests on repeat clients and referrals rather than advertising spend.

How we work

Four commitments we do not negotiate

01

The principal stays on the file

You are not handed to a junior after the first call. The person who understands your constraint is the person who closes the transaction.

02

Documentation before enthusiasm

Title, dues, transferability and approvals are checked before an advance is paid — particularly on authority allotments, where a late discovery is expensive.

03

Charges stated up front

Brokerage and any pass-through costs are agreed at the start. Nothing new appears at the registry table.

04

A candid view, including "no"

If a corridor is priced ahead of what is built, or a file is not clean, we say so. The referral is worth more than the commission.
